🎨 ColorSpark – Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

ColorSpark ("we", "our", or "us") is a kids coloring app. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all users, especially children.

1. Information We Collect

ColorSpark does not collect any personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • We do not have chat, messaging, or social features.
  • Artwork created in the app is saved locally on your device only — we never upload it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

ColorSpark uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain non-personal device information to serve ads, including:

  • Device advertising ID (a resettable anonymous identifier)
  • App usage and interaction data for ad relevance
  • IP address (used for approximate location / regional ads)

Because our app may be used by children, we have configured AdMob to serve child-directed ads in compliance with COPPA and Google's Families Policy. Child-directed ads do not use personalised targeting based on behavioural profiles.

For more information on how Google uses data in ads, please see Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

ColorSpark is designed for children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app requires no registration and stores no personal data on our servers.

4. Data Storage

Any artwork saved within the app is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data and it is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

5. Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — for advertising. Google Privacy Policy

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
